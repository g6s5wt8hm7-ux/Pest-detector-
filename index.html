Yellow Stem Borer

Scirpophaga incertulas

Match Score 93.0%
Damage Threat ⚠️ High Risk
Primary Target Inner Stem & Tillers
Spreading Rate Rapid (Larval phase)

🛡️ Recommended Treatment & Pesticide Dosage

🌿 Organic & Biological Control

  • Release Trichogramma japonicum egg parasitoids at 50,000/ha.
  • Install light traps to attract and collect adult moths.

🧪 Recommended Pesticide

Cartap Hydrochloride 4G

Dosage: 7.5 to 10 kg per acre

Broadcast granular formulations with shallow standing water in paddy field.

Preventive Measures:

Clip seedling tips before transplanting to remove egg masses. Maintain balanced nitrogen fertilizer usage.
